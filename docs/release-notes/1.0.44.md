## The keys a phone's keyboard lacks

Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, Shift, the arrows, Home, End, Page up and Page down,
sent to the field being typed in: a terminal, an SSH app, or a web editor
such as vscode.dev or code-server in the browser. Two Android apps, built
from the same code, which can be on one phone together, and Berpiztu AI
Keyboard for Windows:

- **KeyPlus** (`KeyPlus-Popup.apk`): the keys in a strip right above
  whatever keyboard is open. It works beside the keyboard you already use;
  it does not replace it.
- **Berpiztu AI Keyboard** (`Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard.apk`): a keyboard of its
  own, Gboard's copied, for any Android phone, tablet or headset (Galaxy
  XR).
- **Berpiztu AI Keyboard for Windows** (`Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard-Windows.msi`):
  the same keyboard in a card that floats over every window.

## Berpiztu AI Keyboard, as Gboard

- **Two keyboards**, both in Gboard's look and the device's colours:
  **Estándar**, Gboard's keys, and **Estándar PC**, a PC's (Spanish ISO, or
  US in English): F1–F12, Alt Gr's symbol in each key's corner and on the
  keys while Alt Gr is on, ´ and ` as dead keys, Bloq Mayús, Ctrl, Alt and ⊞
  on until the next key, a PC's keys repeating while held. Each screen
  (phone, tablet upright or on its side, DeX) shows one docked and one
  floating, chosen in **Ajustes › Tema**; a button on the toolbar switches
  between the two, and **Flotante** docks or floats it.
- **Gboard's toolbar and grid** (⊞), in two languages, Spanish and English.
  **⊞ › ✏** customises them as Gboard's: a button held is dragged between
  the toolbar and the grid.
- **Swipe typing**: a word swiped over the letters is typed, read by FUTO
  Swipe; its other likely words wait in the toolbar, and a tap takes one in
  its place.
- **Words as you type**: while a word is typed letter by letter, the
  toolbar offers the commonest words that start so, accents aside
  ("cancio" offers "canción"); a tap writes it with a space after it.
- **🎤 voice typing**: the words appear in the field as they are heard,
  until 🎤 is tapped again or, if **Escribir por voz** says so, after a
  silence. Local is Android's own recognizer; **Red local** is ia-voice
  (NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 ASR) on a computer at home, by its IP; **Remoto** is
  a published one, with the user and password its proxy asks for, kept
  sealed on the device.
- **Emoji**: the comma held, or ⊞'s Emojis, opens them in the keys' place,
  by group, those typed lately first, and the latest in a row over the
  toolbar.
- **⊞ › Diseñar** designs a standard keyboard on the keyboard: a key marked
  is made narrower or wider, dragged elsewhere, or taken out; + Tecla adds
  Esc, Tab, Supr, Inicio, Fin, RePág, AvPág, Ctrl, Alt, ⊞, Alt Gr, F1–F12 or
  an arrow; + Fila adds a row. Hecho saves it as a keyboard of its own, and
  designing it again opens it as saved.
- **Moving out of the way**: a floating keyboard over the field being typed
  in moves above or below it; **Apartarse también de los menús** does the
  same for an open menu, through an accessibility service of the
  keyboard's own that the app turns on by itself once Wireless debugging
  has let it.
- **What was copied** is kept to paste again, images and screenshots too.
- **On the headset** the keyboard is the phone's, as any Android
  keyboard; the headset's own, filling the box the headset gives a
  keyboard, is an option in Preferencias.
- **Both apps turn themselves on** over the device's own Wireless
  debugging, no computer needed.

## Berpiztu AI Keyboard for Windows

- The standard keyboards, Estándar and Estándar PC, in Spanish and English,
  in a card over every window that never takes the focus from the one typed
  in: its keys reach that window as Windows' own.
- Swipe typing, words as you type, emoji in colour, and 🎤 with ia-voice
  (this computer's, one on the local network or a remote one), the words
  typed as they are heard.
- It shows when a text field takes the focus and hides, becomes its ⌨
  icon or stays when the focus leaves; it moves out of the way of the text
  and of open menus; ✕ hides it, and **Salir**, by Windows' clock, ends it.
- A Windows window: moved by its toolbar or handle, made larger or smaller
  at its edges, the rows growing with it.
- Its settings are an app of their own, as on Android: Idiomas,
  Preferencias, Tema, Escritura deslizando el dedo, Escribir por voz and
  Información.

## Where the keyboard stands, from its app

- **Pop-up** puts the keyboard in a window of its own, or docks it again, on
  every screen; an open keyboard takes it at once.
- **Reset the keyboard**, after asking, brings it back as first installed.
- The keyboard's app has a box to try the keys in, clear of Android 15's
  bars, and opens on its two first steps while the keyboard is not on or
  not in use.

## The popup, on a headset and on INAIR's pod

- The popup no longer hangs on INAIR's pod: Android takes five seconds there
  to say where every window is, and that is now asked away from the app's
  main work.
- Its corner grips are large, with diagonal arrows drawn, for a headset's
  pointer reaches them as a finger does.
- KeyPlus turns its own service on where it may write the secure settings.

## Also in this version

- **Built on .NET 10**, with every fix of its latest security release in
  the runtime each app carries inside.
- The popup is hidden while the screen is off.
- A dragged popup follows the finger; a keyboard made in a window from the
  start is measured before it is placed.

## Installing

On Android, download `KeyPlus-Popup.apk`, `Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard.apk` or both
from this page on the phone or the headset, and open them there: each
carries everything it runs, and installs over the version before. On
Windows, run `Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard-Windows.msi`: it installs for you alone,
no administrator asked, starts the keyboard, and starts it out of sight at
every sign-in. The README says how to turn each on.
