## Voice commands, on Android and Windows

While the microphone listens, say "comando" ("command") and what you want, in
Spanish or English:

- **Marks**: coma, punto, punto y coma, dos puntos, interrogación,
  exclamación, comillas, paréntesis, guion, arroba, barra.
- **Capitals**: mayúsculas and minúsculas for the next word, or to turn the
  case of what is selected; bloqueo mayúsculas is the Caps lock key.
- **Lines**: espacio, nueva línea, nuevo párrafo.
- **Deleting**: borra palabra, borra frase, borrar (what is selected),
  deshacer.
- **Selecting and the clipboard**: seleccionar todo, seleccionar párrafo,
  copiar, cortar, pegar; fin and inicio move the caret.
- **Correcting**: ortografía corrects what is selected, or all the text.
- **Actions**: enviar (Enter), parar (the microphone off) and ayuda, a window
  that lists them all.

The command nearest to what was heard is taken, and what the keyboard
understood shows for a moment. Nothing said after "comando" is ever written;
say "comando comando" to write the word.

## The microphone

- **Enter keeps it listening** and starts afresh, on both keyboards; a chat's
  send button too on Android. Any other key stops it at once.
- **Keep transcribing after sending** (Voice typing, off): whether what you
  had said and was not written yet when you send is still written after.
- Its circle is **red while it waits for a voice and blue while you speak**.
- On Android, Local's beeps are silenced; **Sound effects** (Voice typing)
  brings them back.

## Android's spell checker

A language's Spelling has **Corrects with**, one of three: Android's own
spell checker (Google's on most devices), the context model, or Correct
words. The one chosen corrects as you type and for the ortografía command.
**comando gramática** asks Android's spell checker and leaves what it marks
marked.

## Messages where the words are offered

On Android the keyboard's messages show in the line over the toolbar, not
floating on the screen.
