## Use it for suggestions too

On Android, under a language's **Context model**, a new choice, on by
default and turned on again whenever the model is: the words offered as you
type are the model's alone, its three likeliest for the sentence so far.
The word list's come only where the model offers none, so the word in the
centre no longer changes under your finger just before a space takes it.
Off, the words offered are the word list's, as before.

On Windows the model's words are offered the same way; its switch comes
later.
