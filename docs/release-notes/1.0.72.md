## The context model's words

With **Use it for suggestions too** on, the words offered are the model's
alone; the word list's come only where the model gives none. A model taught
with the language in it is asked in the keyboard's, and its words are found
faster, on up to four cores. On Android and Windows.

## Bold, as Gboard

The toolbar's centre goes in bold only where it is sure which word was meant,
and a space writes only a word in bold. With the context model's words, that
is the word a misspelling means ("jente": "gente"), or the likeliest with the
language's new **Percentage for bold** or more, 70 by default. With
Autocorrect, a word nothing of the language starts as shows what it may have
meant. A word only Hunspell knows shows at the left, not to be learnt.

## Choosing a context model

A language's list now offers only the published models that read it, and each
line says at its right whether it is on Hugging Face (a globe) or only on the
device (a computer). The published models have new names: the model, its
languages, Virtus and its bits, as `gemma-es-virtus-8`. Two new ones read
Spanish alone. A model kept but no longer in the folder shows as none.

## Search beams

In **Troubleshooting**: how many answers the context model follows at once for
the words it offers, from 1 to 10, 5 by default. Fewer is faster and may give
worse words; under 3, it may offer fewer than three.

## Debug

A new button of the toolbar: a small window you can move, with the model
chosen, the one really loaded, the Percentage for bold, the search beams and
the words it gives with their percentages.

## Check now, on Android

**About** says under **Check now** the version installed and the latest, on
the page itself, the latest a link to that release on GitHub.

## Also

With Autocorrect, Android corrects in the fields LatinIME does, and a space
writes the toolbar's centre. On Windows, the words offered are also found by
how they sound in Spanish and by the keys beside those typed, and a letter
after a click or a key of the computer's begins a new word. The space bar's
language is as tall as ?123.
