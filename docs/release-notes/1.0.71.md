## Context models from Hugging Face

A language's **Context model** list now holds the correctors Berpiztu
publishes on Hugging Face (berpiztu/keyboard-correctors-gemma and
berpiztu/keyboard-correctors), each with its bits, beside those already on
the device. **Download** fetches the one chosen, every file checked; a Gemma
one shows the Gemma Terms of Use first and asks. A model is turned on only
once it is downloaded. On Android and Windows.

## Use it for suggestions too, on Windows

Under the context model, as on Android: on, the words offered as you type
are the model's; off, the word list's.

## Emoji search

**Search** in the emoji panel finds emoji by their names in the keyboard's
language, Spanish or English. The emoji panel now covers the recent emoji
row and the toolbar too, and the keyboard keeps its height. GIF is gone.

## Fixes

- Android: Preferences' extra line and the other keyboard preferences take
  effect at once, without moving to another field.
- Settings: a download's line sits right under its choice, its button level
  with the others.
