## The keyboard's ✕ follows When the field lets go on Windows

With Become the icon chosen, the ✕ now turns the keyboard into its icon, which
stays until you click it or bring the keyboard back from its icon by the clock;
with Hide, the ✕ hides it.

## The icon by the clock shows and hides the keyboard on Windows

A click on it puts the keyboard away when it is in sight, as its icon or hidden as
When the field lets go says, and brings it back when it is not; the field you were
typing in keeps its focus.
