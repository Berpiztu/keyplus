## The keyboard's ✕ makes it its icon on Windows

Closing the keyboard with its ✕ now turns it into its icon, which stays until you
click it or bring the keyboard back from its icon by the clock.

## The icon by the clock shows and hides the keyboard on Windows

A click on it makes the keyboard its icon when it is in sight, and brings it back
when it is not; the field you were typing in keeps its focus.
