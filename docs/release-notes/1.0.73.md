## The toolbar and the emoji row go with the keys

Made smaller, the keyboard's toolbar and emoji row shrink with its keys, each as
tall as a row of keys, up to their usual height: taller keys leave them there.
On Windows they also stop where the toolbar's buttons fill the keyboard's width,
so none is left out. On Android and Windows; on a headset they stay as they were.

## Larger signs and emoji

The toolbar's signs and the emoji in its row now take 60% of the line's height,
with less room around them.
