## The keys a phone's keyboard lacks

Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, Shift, the arrows, Home, End, Page up and Page down,
sent to the field being typed in: a terminal, an SSH app, or a web editor
such as vscode.dev or code-server in the browser. Two apps, built from the
same code, which can be on one phone together:

- **KeyPlus** (`KeyPlus-Popup.apk`): the keys in a strip right above
  whatever keyboard is open. It works beside the keyboard you already use;
  it does not replace it.
- **Berpiztu AI Keyboard** (`Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard.apk`): the same keys as a
  keyboard of its own, for any Android, and the one for a headset (Galaxy
  XR).

Both keep what was copied to paste again, have Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V,
Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y keys of their own, and turn into a whole Spanish keyboard
with **ABC**, laid out as Windows' touch keyboard lays it out.

## Berpiztu AI Keyboard on a phone, as Gboard

- **What was copied** sits as chips in the title bar, between 📋 and ABC
  and ⚙, and in a row above the keys all the time, tall enough for a text's
  two lines. A long press on a chip opens its menu.
- **⚙** opens its settings in the keys' place, two to a row as Gboard's
  rounded tiles:
  - **Mover** and **Redimensionar**: a frame over the keyboard until
    **Hecho**. A finger held anywhere on it moves it; its top and bottom bars
    size the keys, its side ones a window's width; **Restablecer** brings
    back the usual size.
  - **Modo normal**: docked, and raised as far as Mover left it.
  - **Modo ventana**: a window of its own over the app, which keeps the whole
    screen and the touches outside the keyboard.
- **Editar**'s card stands above the keyboard, over the app, on one line
  that never grows.
- A **long press** moves the keyboard. With a mouse or a controller, the
  pointer turns into diagonal arrows near a corner, and a press there
  resizes it.
- The keys stay clear of the navigation bar.

## Where the keyboard stands, from its app

- **Pop-up** puts the keyboard in a window of its own, or docks it again, on
  every screen; an open keyboard takes it at once.
- **Reset the keyboard**, after asking, brings it back as first installed.
- **Make it the keyboard in use** does it by itself where the app may write
  the secure settings, and **Copy the ADB commands** copies what an ADB app
  on the device runs once to let it.
- The keyboard's screen has a box to try the keys in, clear of Android 15's
  bars.

## The popup, on a headset and on INAIR's pod

- The popup no longer hangs on INAIR's pod: Android takes five seconds there
  to say where every window is, and that is now asked away from the app's
  main work. Window changes that come together are placed once, and a
  display that refused the window is left alone for a minute.
- Its corner grips are large, with diagonal arrows drawn, for a headset's
  pointer reaches them as a finger does.
- KeyPlus turns its own service on where it may write the secure settings.

## Installing

Download `KeyPlus-Popup.apk`, `Berpiztu-AI-Keyboard.apk` or both from this
page on the phone or the headset, and open them there: each carries
everything it runs, and installs over the version before. The README says
how to turn each on.
