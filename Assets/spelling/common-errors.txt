list (Wikipedia:Corrector ortográfico/Listado): each entry a line starting with a
space, "written|parameter|meant, meant", the likeliest first; cs as the parameter
matches the case too. Those its list lacks, or lists with fewer of the words meant.
A word written that is a word too (as, tubo) is only offered, never corrected alone.
